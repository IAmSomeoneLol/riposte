# Riposte
Riposte is a Parry Mod that is based off a video titled “[Essence of PARRY](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9NPbOl0LGds)” a knock-off standalone variant made for the Fabric version, from mod [Terramity](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/terramity) (owned by [Aurukel](https://www.curseforge.com/members/aurukel/projects)) done under a Comission request. 

![ripostebanner](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/2a418daef632186f07359d654469604d994f7002.png)

## Parry Uses

### Projectile Deflect
You can **Parry the Projectile** BACK towards the entity (You must aim with your crosshair) Only if a Parry slot with **Projectile Deflect** is active.

![projectileparry](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/dMV6OgNj/images/28b1064934cd36dbca20d3b9e739ae58fc0a581e.gif)

### Parry of FABRIC
And the normal Parry. When parrying you deal a knockback to make a gap between the entity and yourself. Also offering you for a **Kick Combo** to hit them further away (must be done quickly as soon as parry is executed)

![normal parry](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/dMV6OgNj/images/e1454f244cadc5414bfbd0cebe1f298f08eb6b2e.gif)

If a weapon has sweeping edge and is executed with a kick combo, the entities inflicted by the sweeping will also have the effects applied of the Kick Combo knockback.

![parrycombo](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/dMV6OgNj/images/f518f7715a516c3ad9eba5100f6d69f05bc1cac7.gif)

The parry will not restrict you from attacking or any other movement. With right equipment **equipped* you're able to parry fall damage and even void or /kill command itself.

![heightparry](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/dMV6OgNj/images/cf0dd245d56323fab64deda882842e65cc35c6a2.gif)

## Server Options

The mod was made very accessable and easy to edit values, along with disabling and enabling some features fit to your accomidations.

### Some settings include such as:

- **Multi-Parry** allowing you to parry as long as you want during your Parry active window length.

- **Allow Parry while Using item** (self explanatory) You cannot execute parries whilst eating/blocking shield and so on.

- **Enforce Knockback** allows all entities to have knockback dealt by parry such as Iron golems, Wardens, etc.

- **Deflect Projectiles** allows you to parry backwards the projectile depending on where you aim with the crosshair.

- **Kick Combo** is a After-parry mechanic, when an entity is parried, attack them in a small window frame to send them back even further.

- **Hitstop** freezes the entity for some time before sending them away (used to make kick combo easier)

![ServerOptions](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/c0d1b51f9e7933947ccbc18d9718b77ea3b15e8e.png)

## Client Options

- **Parry Activation** of parry, being either Keybind (default) or Camera (right click on an entity in range to parry).

- **And many more settings** for client (QOL as like turning flash to Black instead of white, or completely off, FOV effects, recoil, particle on and off, sounds, icon, etc.

![clientoption](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/a3076de2c2d129596f7357871e9806e91f1bbb04.png)

**Available in:** Village Weaponsmiths, Village Armorers, Village Toolsmiths, Ruined Portals, Stronghold Corridors. (15% common)

**When worn**
- Allows you to parry Projectile damage
- +3 Parry Recharge rate

**Available in:** End Cities (5% Rare)

**When worn**

- +3 Parry Recharge rate

**Available in:** Stronghold Corridors, Stronghold Crossings, Ancient Cities, End Cities (5% Rare)

- Allows you to Parry Projectile damage

## Dependencies
As of now only mc1.20.1v Fabric loader is supported.

- [Fabric API](https://modrinth.com/mod/fabric-api)
- [FZFY Config](https://modrinth.com/mod/fzzy-config)
- [Trinkets](https://modrinth.com/mod/trinkets)
- [Player Animator](https://modrinth.com/mod/player-animator)
- [Mod Menu](https://modrinth.com/mod/modmenu) (Used for Accessing the config client side)

The mod will Recieve small time support (Bug fixes, Visual Effects, Animations,..) overtime until it's perfected and be less clunky than it is now.
